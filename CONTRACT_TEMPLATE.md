# 📄 Contract Template — Website Development Services

## ⚠️ IMPORTANT: If You're Under 18

### **Can you legally sign a contract as a minor?**

**Short answer:** Yes, BUT with restrictions:

1. **In Romania:** Minors aged 14-18 CAN sign contracts for work/services, but:
   - Your parents/legal guardian should know about it
   - For larger amounts (>1000 Lei), parent approval is recommended
   - Parent can "ratify" (approve) the contract to make it stronger

2. **Under 14:** Parent/guardian MUST sign on your behalf

3. **Best Practice (safest):** Have your parent co-sign the contract below

---

## ✅ When You CAN Work as a Minor:

- ✅ Freelance web development (like this project)
- ✅ Creating websites, apps, graphic design
- ✅ Selling digital products
- ✅ Small contracts with friends or family businesses

## 🚫 What You CAN'T Do as a Minor:

- ❌ Sign long-term employment contracts (need 18+)
- ❌ Register as PFA (Persoană Fizică Autorizată) - need 18+
- ❌ Open business bank accounts alone
- ❌ Sign contracts with large companies without parent approval

## 🚨 Special Notes for Minors:

### **Do You Need This Contract?**

**IF the work is FREE (between friends):**
- Contract is OPTIONAL but strongly recommended
- It protects you even if no money involved
- Shows you're serious and professional

**IF you're charging money:**
- Contract is HIGHLY RECOMMENDED
- Your parent should definitely know and sign
- Proves the agreement if there's any dispute later

### **Can Your Friend Pay You If You're a Minor?**

✅ **YES, legally he can pay you for work, BUT:**

1. **Small amounts (<1000 Lei):** Totally fine, just cash or bank transfer
2. **Larger amounts (>1000 Lei):** Recommended to:
   - Have parent know about it
   - Pay into parent's bank account (since you might not have one)
   - Or pay cash with signed receipt

3. **Tax implications:**
   - In Romania, income <600 Lei/month is usually tax-free
   - Above that, you might need to report it (parent handles this)
   - For friend projects, usually no issue
