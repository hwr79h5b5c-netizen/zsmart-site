# 🛡️ Legal Protection Guide — Website Developer

## Your Situation:
You built this website for your friend's company (ZSmart Distribution ZTG SRL). You need to protect yourself from any liability related to their business operations.

---

## ✅ What's Already Done:

### 1. **Website Disclaimer (Footer)**
Added clear disclaimer stating:
- AI assistant provides suggestions only, not professional diagnosis
- Website developed by independent contractor
- All legal correspondence must go to ZSmart Distribution ZTG SRL directly
- Developer has no responsibility for business operations or customer complaints

**Location:** `index.html` footer section  
**Visibility:** Always visible at bottom of every page

---

## 📋 Additional Steps You Should Take:

### **2. Create a Written Agreement (Contract)**

**See `CONTRACT_TEMPLATE.md` for the full contract!**

Two versions available:
- **Version A:** If you're under 18 (includes parent signature)
- **Version B:** If you're 18+ (simpler, no parent needed)

The contract clearly states:
- What you built (just the website)
- What you're NOT responsible for (business operations, AI accuracy, customer complaints)
- Protection clause (your friend agrees to protect you from lawsuits)
- Works even if the project is FREE (between friends)

---

### **3. Keep Communication Records**

Save all communication with your friend:
- ✅ Emails discussing the project
- ✅ Screenshots of messages
- ✅ Any agreements on what you're building
- ✅ Confirmation that disclaimers are acceptable

**Why:** If someone sues, you can prove you warned about AI limitations and clarified your role.

---

### **4. Document Everything Technical**

Create a handover document (I can help you with this) stating:

- What you built
- What you DID NOT build (business operations, warranties, service quality)
- Known limitations of the AI
- Maintenance responsibilities (is your friend responsible for updates?)

---

### **5. Insurance (Optional but Recommended)**

If you do freelance work regularly, consider:
- **Professional Indemnity Insurance** (covers errors in your work)
- **Cyber Liability Insurance** (covers data breaches, hacking)

Cost: ~€150-300/year in Romania
